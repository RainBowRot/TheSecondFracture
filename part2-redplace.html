The threshold

The Red Places

Beyond the Garden’s water, the world is not green.

The river that had always known their names ended in a red that did not remember them kindly. A drowned cathedral waited at the far side of the dark, its windows lit from within like a wound that had learned how to pray. Black stone. Blood glass. A path that only went down.

This is where Vespera came from.

This is where Asmodeus had hunted her.

This is where the three of them would now have to walk — Seraphine’s remade light still new in her bones, and the Garden already too far behind to call her back.

The red places did not pretend they were not listening.

They had been waiting.
