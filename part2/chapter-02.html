II

The First Small Fracture

It began so quietly that none of them named it at first.

Seraphine sat between Asmodeus’s knees in the red dark, her white horns brushing his chest, one hand resting over the dark ink of his heart. He held her with the easy possessiveness of someone who had finally stopped chasing. Her eyes were closed. Her body had already learned the shape of safety in his arms.

Vespera stood a few paces away and watched.

The bond still pulsed between all three of them — bright, living, irreversible. But something in the way Seraphine leaned into him, the way his hand settled at the small of her back as if it had always belonged there, made the demoness feel the first thin edge of distance. She had brought them here. She had chosen this. And yet the circle that had once felt equally shared was beginning, almost imperceptibly, to tighten around two instead of three.

Asmodeus looked up and met her eyes over Seraphine’s white hair. There was no cruelty in his gaze. Only the quiet knowledge that something had already started to shift.

Vespera did not speak.

She only stood in the red light with her wings half-open and felt the first small fracture form inside the claim she had once believed was permanent.
