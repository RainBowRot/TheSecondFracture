The Decision That Does Not Speak Its Name

They stood at the edge of the abyss and did not look at each other.

Below them the Red Places fell away into a deeper dark than even Asmodeus had names for. The city of blood-light and black stone stretched to the horizon, beautiful and merciless. Vespera felt the wind of the depths move through her hair and knew, with the quiet certainty of someone who had already begun to leave, that the circle of three was no longer the place she belonged.

Asmodeus’s hand rested at the small of her back. Not claiming. Not yet releasing. Simply present. He had spent centuries hunting her. Now that she was finally still, he could feel the moment she started to slip away again — and this time he did not know if he was meant to stop her.

Neither of them spoke the decision out loud.

It lived in the space between their bodies, in the way Vespera’s shoulders had already turned slightly toward the dark, in the way Asmodeus’s hand did not tighten to keep her. The bond still pulsed. It simply no longer asked her to stay.
