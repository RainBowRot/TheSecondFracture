VIII

What Refuses to Thin

In the red water they tried to make the bond forget her.

Seraphine wrapped herself around Asmodeus as if closeness alone could erase the third name still written under their skin. The blood-light of the Red Places moved through the water around them, soft and endless. Her white horns rested against his. His hands held her as if she were the only claim that had ever mattered. For a long time neither of them spoke. The silence was almost enough.

Almost.

Even here, even with the water holding them and the red dark sealing the world away, the bond refused to thin completely. Somewhere beneath the heat of their bodies a quieter pulse still answered to a third heartbeat that was no longer present. Seraphine felt it and pressed closer, as if she could smother the memory with her own. Asmodeus felt it and did not name it.

They stayed in the water until the light around them dimmed. Until the need to replace what had been lost became the only language left between them. Until the absence itself began to feel like a presence they could no longer ignore.
