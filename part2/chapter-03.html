III

What the Bond Begins to Prefer

The bond did not break.

It simply began to prefer.

Seraphine remained close to Asmodeus in the red dark, her body turned toward him as if the space between them had already decided it was no longer necessary. Her hand rested over his heart. His arm stayed around her. The white of her wings and the dark of his formed a quiet, closed shape that left little room for a third.

Vespera stood at the edge of that shape and felt the distance grow by inches she could not name. The claim still lived in all three of them — she could feel it under her skin, the same red pulse that had sealed them under the willow. But the pulse no longer reached her with the same strength. It had begun to circle tighter around the two who sat together, leaving her outside the warmth she had once helped create.

She did not accuse them. She only watched, red eyes steady, and understood that the first true fracture was not going to come from an enemy.

It was going to come from the way love itself could choose.
