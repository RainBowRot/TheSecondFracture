What We Carry

They walked into a forest that had never known the Garden’s light or the Red Places’ blood.

Bioluminescent flowers opened around their feet. Neon petals drifted through the dark like slow stars. Asmodeus carried her as if the centuries of hunting had always been leading to this single act of keeping. Vespera rested against him, no longer running, no longer waiting to be claimed by something that would eventually choose another.

The bond between them was quieter now. Thinner. Honest. It no longer pretended to hold three. It simply held what remained.

Somewhere far from the glowing trees, in a mortal world that had never heard their names, an angel who had once worn horns and called herself more than light began to remember what had been taken from her. The memory did not feel like grief.

It felt like the first spark of something that would one day learn how to burn.

But that is another story.

Some fractures cannot be mended.
Some were never meant to be.
And still — we chose to carry what remained.
