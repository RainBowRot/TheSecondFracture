XI

The Last Place She Felt Loved

He found her where he always knew she would go.

Under the same willow. In the same soft light. The Garden had not rejected her this time. It had simply waited, the way it waited for all broken things that still remembered how to return. Vespera sat against the trunk with petals in her hair and a single tear still drying on her cheek. She did not look surprised when his shadow fell across her.

Asmodeus knelt.

He did not speak at first. He only held out his hand the way a man offers a claim that has already survived worse than this. The red light of the bond — thinner now, but still alive — moved between them like a quiet recognition.

Vespera’s voice was rough. “You sent her away.”

“I corrected what was starving us.”

She looked at his hand for a long time. Then, slowly, she placed her fingers in his. The willow above them stirred once, as if it remembered the three who had once stood here and the two who had returned.

“I never stopped belonging to you,” she said.

Asmodeus closed his hand around hers. “I know. That is why I came back.”
