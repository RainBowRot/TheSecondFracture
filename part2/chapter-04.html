IV

The Space She Is No Longer Given

They stood side by side at the edge of the red water and did not speak.

Seraphine’s white wings and Vespera’s dark ones almost touched, but the small space between them had become something heavier than air. Below them the flooded halls of the Red Places stretched into darkness, glowing with the same blood-light that now lived under all three of their skins. The beauty of it was undeniable. The loneliness of it was new.

Vespera kept her eyes on the water. She could feel Seraphine’s presence the way she had always been able to feel the borders of the Garden — as something that used to open for her and now quietly closed. The bond still existed. It simply no longer made room.

Seraphine’s voice, when it came, was soft. “You brought us here.”

“I did.”

“And now you stand as if you are already leaving.”

Vespera did not deny it. She only watched the red lights move across the water and felt the claim that had once held three begin to forget the shape of her name.
