XII

What We Will Make Instead

He lifted her as if she weighed nothing.

Vespera’s arms went around his neck. Her face pressed into the dark ink over his heart. The willow watched them the way it had watched the beginning of everything — without judgment, without hope, only the quiet knowledge that some returns are permanent.

Asmodeus carried her away from the Garden for the last time. Not back to the Red Places that had once been exile. Not toward any claim that required a third to complete it. Toward something neither of them had names for yet — a place that would belong only to the two of them, built from what remained after the fracture.

She did not ask where they were going.

She only held on, and let the centuries of running finally end in the arms of the one who had never stopped hunting her, and who had finally learned how to keep without conquering.

Behind them the willow continued its slow weeping.

Ahead of them, the path opened into a darkness that felt, for the first time, like home.
