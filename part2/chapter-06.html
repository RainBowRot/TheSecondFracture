VI

The Leaving

She left the way she had always left — alone, and without permission.

The flooded halls of the Red Places held her reflection in broken pieces. Water rose around her ankles, cold and red-lit. A single tear tracked through the dark makeup beneath her eye and fell into the water without a sound. Her wings hung half-open, not ready for flight, only for the weight of what she was walking away from.

The bond still lived under her skin. It pulsed once, twice, as if asking her to turn around. She did not. She had felt the circle close without her. She had watched the preference form. She had stood at the edge of the abyss and understood that staying would only make the fracture deeper.

So she did the only thing she had ever known how to do when love became too sharp to hold.

She left.

Behind her the Red Places continued to glow. Ahead of her the long dark waited, and somewhere far above, the Garden she had once fled still held the shape of a willow that had known all three of their names.
