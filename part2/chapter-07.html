VII

The Narrowing

With Vespera gone, the bond did not break.

It narrowed.

Seraphine pressed closer to Asmodeus in the red dark, her forehead against his, her hand over the dark ink of his heart as if she could hold the remaining claim in place by touch alone. His arms closed around her without hesitation. The space that had once held three now held only two, and the silence left behind was both relief and wound.

She kissed him with a new urgency — not the soft learning of the Garden, but the sharp need of someone trying to seal a door that had already opened. Her white horns brushed his. Her body fitted against his as if the third presence had never existed. For a moment the bond answered her, bright and exclusive, and she almost believed she could make it forget the one who had left.

Asmodeus held her through it. He did not push her away. He also did not pretend the absence was nothing.

When they finally stilled, Seraphine’s voice was barely more than breath. “She is gone.”

“I know,” he said.

And the red light around them continued to burn as if nothing had been lost at all.
