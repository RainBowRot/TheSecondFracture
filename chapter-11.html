Chapter XI

What We Keep

The last of the old laws had already failed.

What remained was not a choice between light and hunger. It was three mouths, three bodies, and a Garden that no longer knew how to look away.

Seraphine kept them both.
They kept her.

The borders would remember this. The red places beyond the water were already listening.

Book One closes here.
The second fracture is only the door.
