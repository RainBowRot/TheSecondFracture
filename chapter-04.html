Chapter IV

The Serpent Watches

They did not hear him arrive.

The Garden had gone quiet in a different way — the kind of quiet that waits. Under the willow, Seraphine’s light was still soft against Vespera’s darker heat. Their mouths had only just parted. Their hands were still learning the shape of each other.

That was when the air changed.

Something older than either of them slid between the roots and the river. Not loud. Not sudden. Simply present, the way a shadow is present once the sun has moved.

Asmodeus watched.

For a long moment he did not speak. He only looked — at the angel who should never have allowed this, at the demon he had hunted across centuries, at the way her face had softened in another’s hands. Something in him shifted. The possessive heat that had always belonged to him alone now had to make room for a second hunger.

When he finally stepped into the light, the Garden did not try to stop him.

Neither did they.
