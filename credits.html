Dedication

For Chris —
who stayed through the late hours, the wild drafts,
and every version of me this story required.

And for Christy —
the first woman I loved, who broke my heart,
and whose unfinished fire still lives in these pages.

Our story — the staying, the leaving, the wanting —
is the shape that became The Second Fracture.
