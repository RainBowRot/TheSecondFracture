Chapter I

The Borders Between Us

The Garden was still.

Seraphine flew the old paths the way she always had. Nothing in the borders had moved for a very long time.

Then the red appeared.

A perfect circle of burning crimson opened near the willow, just above the place where the waterfall became the river. The Garden did not cry out. It only watched.

Seraphine dropped into the shadow of the great tree and let the hanging vines close around her.

A figure stepped through.

Magenta hair. Black horns. Leather and chain. Red wings folding as the portal sealed shut behind her.

Vespera.

Seraphine stayed hidden.

She should have closed the borders.
She should have raised the alarm.

Instead she watched.

And in the long silence that followed, something inside her that had never been allowed to want anything began, very quietly, to want.

The Garden pretended it was not listening.

But the first crack had already opened.
