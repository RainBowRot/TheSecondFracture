About Rainbow Rot

Rainbow Rot is a house of games, assets, and dark romance — stories and worlds that treat desire as weather, and color as something that can rot and still be beautiful.

We build graphic novels the way old gardens keep secrets: slowly, in the dark, and with more beauty than is strictly safe. We also make 2D and 3D game assets, psychedelic fantasy, and the long-form game that gives the house its name.

The Second Fracture is a sister volume to Crimson Veil: another night that refuses to end, another set of borders that fail for love. Both live under The Veil, the romance arm of Rainbow Rot.
