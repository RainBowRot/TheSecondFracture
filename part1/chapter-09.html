Chapter IX

Blood & Promise

They sealed it in blood.

Asmodeus drew a thin line across his own palm first. Then Vespera’s. Then Seraphine’s. Three cuts. Three mouths. They pressed the wounds together until the red light of the bond moved between them, bright and living, until it was no longer possible to tell where one ended and the others began.

The blood did not simply mix. It remembered. It carried the centuries of hunting, the centuries of guarding, the stolen hours under the willow, the moment the light softened and the horns rose. When the joining was complete, the mark settled beneath their skin — not a scar, but a quiet, permanent pulse that only the three of them could feel.

Seraphine looked down at her own hand. The gold that used to live there was gone. In its place was something older and more honest. She raised her eyes to the two who had remade her and found no regret waiting.

Vespera’s voice was soft. “You are ours now. Fully.”

Asmodeus’s hand closed over both of theirs. “And we are yours.”

The willow watched the final sealing in silence. The borders of the Garden no longer mattered. The claim had become the only law any of them still answered to.
