Chapter VII

After

The quiet that followed was heavier than the claiming.

They lay tangled in the soft dark beneath the willow, the lanterns burned down to embers, petals stuck to damp skin. Seraphine’s head rested on Asmodeus’s chest. Vespera’s leg was thrown over both of them, her magenta hair spilled across the angel’s white wing. No one spoke for a long time.

The bond hummed between them like a second heartbeat. Three bodies. One closed circle. The Garden had not rejected them. It had simply gone silent, as if it no longer knew what to do with what they had become.

Seraphine traced the dark ink over Asmodeus’s heart with one finger. “I can still feel the light,” she whispered. “But it does not answer the same way.”

Vespera kissed the curve of her shoulder. “It is learning a new language. So are you.”

Asmodeus’s hand moved slowly up and down Seraphine’s spine, protective and possessive at once. “There is no going back from this,” he said. Not as a threat. As a fact.

None of them disagreed.

Outside the circle of their bodies the willow continued to drip its soft green light, and somewhere far below the surface the Red Places stirred, sensing that one of their own had finally stopped running.
