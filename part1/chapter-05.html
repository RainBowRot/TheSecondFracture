Chapter V

Three Mouths

The fruit was only the beginning.

Asmodeus held the ripe, golden thing between two fingers and brought it first to Vespera’s lips. She took the bite without looking away from him. Juice ran down her chin. He caught it with his thumb, then offered the same fruit to Seraphine.

The angel hesitated only a second. Then she leaned forward and tasted what the demoness had already claimed. The juice was sweet and dark and wrong in the most deliberate way. It stained her mouth the same red as Vespera’s eyes.

Asmodeus watched them both with something like reverence and hunger mixed together. When Seraphine finished the bite he pulled her closer by the waist and kissed the remaining juice from her lips. Then he turned and did the same to Vespera. Three mouths. One hunger. The slow, irreversible mathematics of three becoming one closed circle under the green light of the willow.

Seraphine’s halo flickered. Not in warning this time. In surrender.

Vespera’s hand found the angel’s wing and stroked the pure white feathers as if she were already mourning them. Asmodeus’s mouth moved from one throat to the other, leaving no space between them for doubt.

They did not speak of borders again that night.

There was no longer any point.
