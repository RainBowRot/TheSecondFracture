Chapter X

What Remains

Morning found them still joined.

The blood had dried on their palms. The lanterns had burned out. Petals lay scattered across skin and moss like a soft, forgotten offering. They rose together under the willow — red wings, dark wings, and the new iridescent pearl of what Seraphine had become — and for a moment the Garden almost looked as if it might forgive them.

It did not.

But it did not stop them either.

Seraphine stood between the two who had claimed her, white horns catching the first real light of day, and felt the bond pulse steady and sure beneath her skin. There was no halo left to lose. No border left to guard. Only the three of them and the quiet knowledge that whatever came next would be faced together.

Vespera’s hand found hers. Asmodeus’s hand closed over both.

Behind them the willow continued its slow weeping. Ahead of them the path that led away from the Garden (and toward the deeper red) waited without judgment.

They did not look back.

Some fractures cannot be mended.
Some were never meant to be.
And still — they chose to carry what remained.
