Final Plates

They left the Garden together.

The willow kept the shape of what it could no longer hold. The borders, once absolute, simply stood open behind them. No voice called them back. No light tried to reclaim what it had already lost.

Ahead of them the path darkened and deepened, leading toward the place Vespera had once fled and Asmodeus had always ruled. The Red Places waited — not as exile this time, but as the next room of the house they were building with their own hands.

Seraphine walked between them, white horns catching the last of the Garden’s light, and did not look behind her.

Some fractures cannot be mended.
Some were never meant to be.
And still — they chose to carry what remained.

The Red Places

Far below the surface of any garden, the Red Places wait — a drowned cathedral of blood-light and stone, where the air itself remembers every claim that was ever made and never released. This is where the second fracture will be tested.
