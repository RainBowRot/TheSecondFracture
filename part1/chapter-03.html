Chapter III

Where Light Softens

The light did not leave her all at once.

It softened. It hesitated. It watched the way Vespera’s hands mapped the places no angel was supposed to be touched and did not stop them. Seraphine’s wings remained white. Her horns had not yet risen. But the gold in her skin had begun to listen to a different voice.

They returned to the willow every night. The borders no longer tried to keep Vespera out. The wards simply… forgot her. Or chose not to remember. Seraphine would find her already waiting beneath the lanterns, magenta hair loose, red eyes patient and hungry, and every time the angel stepped into her arms the fracture deepened a little more.

Vespera never rushed her. She kissed the inside of Seraphine’s wrists. The hollow of her throat. The soft place just beneath the glow of the halo. Each touch was deliberate, as if she were teaching the light itself how to want.

“You are still so careful,” Vespera whispered one night, lips against the angel’s collarbone. “Even now.”

Seraphine’s fingers tightened in her hair. “I do not know how to be anything else.”

“Then let me show you.”

The light around them dimmed further. Not extinguished. Just softer. Warmer. Almost human. And for the first time, Seraphine did not fight the change.
