What We Carry

Some fractures cannot be mended.
Some were never meant to be.

And still — we chose to carry what remained.

The Garden did not ask them to be whole. It only asked them to keep the change. Vespera and Asmodeus walked the old paths with a third name between them, and the borders, which had never known what to do with three, learned how to hold two who refused to forget.

The second fracture did not close.

It became the way they loved.
