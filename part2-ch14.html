XIV

Only Us

They kept each other because that was what she had asked.

Vespera’s face against his throat. His mouth in her hair. Wings closing until the Garden could not see anything but the two of them, and even then it watched. There was no triumph in it. There was the old hunger, and the newer grief, and the stubborn fact that they were still here.

Only us.

The words did not fit. Seraphine was in the bond on their palms, in the way Vespera cried without sound, in the way Asmodeus held on as if letting go would finish the unmaking the red had started.

The Garden kept its silence.

It had recorded the cost. It would not give the cost back.

They stayed under the willow until the light changed, two shadows carrying a third, and did not speak of the water again.
