III

The Cathedral

Black stone. Blood glass. Lanterns that burned without mercy.

Seraphine rested against him on the cathedral floor, white silk pooled in the dark, her new horns catching the same light that had once been forbidden to her. Asmodeus’s arm stayed around her as if the building itself might try to take her back. Vespera remained standing, red wings half-open, watching the two of them the way the Garden had once watched her.

She had wanted them both here.

She had not wanted to see how naturally Seraphine fit against a creature who had never been gentle with anyone but the two of them.

“The path only goes down,” Vespera said at last.

Asmodeus’s eyes lifted to hers.

“Then we go down.”

Seraphine did not speak. She only kept her hand on him, and the cathedral, which had never loved light, learned the shape of her anyway.
