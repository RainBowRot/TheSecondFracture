IV

What She Sees

They left him behind for a little while.

Vespera led Seraphine to the flooded arcade, where the red water held the windows like a second sky. They stood together on the black stone and looked. Pearl wing and membrane wing. White silk and black lace. The two of them, for a moment, without his claim between their bodies.

“This is what I was,” Vespera said. “Before the Garden. Before you.”

Seraphine’s voice was very quiet. “It is looking back.”

It was. The water did not merely reflect. It regarded. It took the measure of the remade angel the way the Garden had once taken the measure of a demon who had slipped the borders.

Vespera’s hand found Seraphine’s without turning.

“Do not let it decide you,” she whispered.

Seraphine did not promise.
