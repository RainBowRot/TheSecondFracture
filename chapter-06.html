Chapter VI

What We Claim

There was a moment when any of them could have stopped.

None of them did.

Asmodeus’s mouth found Vespera’s first — deep, claiming, the kind of kiss that carried centuries of unfinished hunger. Seraphine watched for only a breath before she leaned in, her light brushing both of them. When Vespera’s hand slid into her hair and Asmodeus’s arm pulled her closer, the last careful distance between the three of them disappeared.

What followed was slow at first.

Hands mapped skin that had never been meant to be shared. Silk was pushed aside. Leather and chain gave way under more than one set of fingers. Seraphine’s soft sounds against Asmodeus’s mouth drew a darker answer from him; Vespera’s teeth at his throat drew an answering heat from both. They moved together under the willow like something that had always been inevitable — angel light softening against demon heat, demon hunger finally given somewhere to go that did not require destruction.

The claiming was not rushed.

It was thorough.

Mouths, hands, bodies learning the exact shape of one another until there was no part of the moment left unclaimed. Seraphine’s wings trembled. Vespera’s red wings half-spread as if to hold the three of them inside the secret. Asmodeus held them both with the possessive certainty of a creature who had spent centuries hunting only to discover he would rather keep than conquer.

When it was over, the air under the willow felt permanently altered.

They stayed tangled in the quiet that followed — foreheads touching, hands still resting on skin that now belonged to more than one hunger. No one spoke. No one moved away.

The Garden kept its silence.

But the borders had already decided something irreversible had been taken… and would not be given back.
