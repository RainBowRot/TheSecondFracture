Chapter II

Stolen Hours

The portal closed.

Vespera stood in the soft light of the Garden as if she had always belonged there. Seraphine stepped out from the willow’s shadow.

Neither spoke.

The waterfall kept falling. Petals drifted between them like slow, red snow. For a long moment the only sound was the river and the quiet, dangerous fact that an angel and a demon were breathing the same air.

Seraphine should have sent her back.
Vespera should have already gone.

Instead they stayed.

What happened under the willow was not yet a touch and not yet a fall. It was the first stolen hour — two bodies that were never meant to share the same ground, choosing to remain on it. Eyes held. Breath shortened. The Garden pretended it was looking elsewhere.

By the time the light shifted, something irreversible had already begun.

They had not crossed every line.
They had only refused to leave.
