Chapter III

Where Light Softens

They did not mean to cross it.

One moment Vespera’s hand was still on Seraphine’s face, steadying her. The next, the distance between their mouths disappeared.

The kiss was quiet at first — almost careful — as if both of them were waiting for the Garden to finally object. It never did. The waterfall kept falling. The petals kept drifting. The only thing that changed was the way Seraphine’s light seemed to soften against the darker heat of the demon holding her.

What began as a single stolen touch became something neither of them could name. Hands found silk and leather. Wings shifted. Breath turned uneven. Under the willow they learned the shape of each other’s mouths and the dangerous fact that neither of them wanted to stop.

When they finally pulled apart, the air between them felt thinner than it had before.

Something irreversible had been spoken without words.

The Garden still pretended it was not listening.

But the light around Seraphine no longer looked the same.
