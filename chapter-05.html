Chapter V

Three Mouths

He did not take her from the angel.

He stepped into the space they had already made and waited to see if either of them would refuse him.

Neither did.

Asmodeus’s hand closed around the back of Seraphine’s neck, thumb brushing the edge of her halo. When she only shivered and stayed, something older and far more dangerous uncoiled in him. Vespera watched with open hunger, her mouth already parted, as if she had been waiting for this exact permission.

What followed was slow and deliberate.

Asmodeus kissed the angel first — deep, claiming, tasting the soft light that had never been meant for him. Seraphine made a quiet sound against his mouth that went straight through both demons. Vespera’s hands slid under the sheer white silk at the same moment Asmodeus’s free hand found the leather and chains on Vespera’s body. Mouths traded. Teeth grazed. Breath turned uneven and shared.

Seraphine ended up between them, light and heat and the dangerous fact that she was no longer only an angel in this moment. Vespera’s mouth found her throat. Asmodeus’s mouth found Vespera’s. Three sets of hands learned the shape of skin that had never been meant to be touched by more than one kind of hunger.

The Garden kept its silence.

Under the willow, three mouths discovered a shared want that none of them were willing to end.
