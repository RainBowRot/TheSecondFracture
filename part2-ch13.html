XIII

Garden Air

Green again — and nothing simple about it.

The willow was where they had left it. The waterfall continued its quiet work. Petals fell as if they had not noticed a world ending on the other side of the water.

Vespera sat in the roots with her knees drawn up and the red still drying out of her lashes. Asmodeus sat facing her, close enough to reach, and for a long time he did not.

Garden air hurt.

It was too soft. It remembered Seraphine too accurately. It did not know how to hold two demons who had come back with a third name and no third mouth.

He offered his hand.

She looked at it the way Seraphine had looked at it in the red — as a border, as a cost, as the only road left.

When she finally let him close the distance, it was not forgiveness.

It was remaining.
